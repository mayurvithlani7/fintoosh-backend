# Privacy Policy for Fintoosh (Dhan Ki Samajh)

**Last Updated: November 1, 2025**

## Introduction

Welcome to Fintoosh ("Dhan Ki Samajh"), a family financial education app designed to help parents teach children about money management, saving, and financial responsibility. We are committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of all our users, especially children under the age of 13.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. By using Fintoosh, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) Compliance

**Fintoosh is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** and other applicable privacy laws. We are committed to protecting children's privacy online.

### Key COPPA Commitments:
- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent
- Parents have full control over their children's accounts and data
- We provide parents with access to review and delete their children's personal information
- We do not share children's personal information with third parties for marketing purposes

## Information We Collect

### Information Provided by Parents:
- **Email address** - For account creation and password recovery
- **Full name** - For account personalization
- **Mobile number** - For account security (optional, not required)
- **Family relationship information** - To establish family connections

### Information Provided by Children (with Parental Consent):
- **Username** - For child account identification
- **PIN** - For child account security
- **Avatar/character preferences** - For personalization
- **App usage data** - Learning progress and achievements

### Information Collected Automatically:
- **Device information** - Device type, operating system, app version
- **Usage analytics** - App features used, time spent, performance metrics
- **Crash reports** - To improve app stability and user experience

### Financial Learning Data:
- **Virtual points/money** - Educational financial transactions
- **Goals and achievements** - Learning progress tracking
- **Chores and rewards** - Family activity management
- **Savings patterns** - Educational insights (aggregated and anonymized)

## How We Use Your Information

### For Parents:
- Account management and authentication
- Communication about account status and updates
- Providing customer support
- Analyzing app usage to improve features

### For Children (with Parental Consent):
- Creating personalized learning experiences
- Tracking educational progress
- Providing age-appropriate content and challenges
- Generating family financial insights

### General Purposes:
- Maintaining app functionality and security
- Improving user experience and app performance
- Complying with legal obligations
- Preventing fraud and abuse

## Parental Consent and Control

### Before creating child accounts, parents must:
1. Create their own verified parent account
2. Explicitly consent to data collection for their children
3. Accept responsibility for monitoring their children's app usage

### Parents can:
- **Review** all information collected about their children
- **Delete** their children's accounts and data at any time
- **Modify** account settings and permissions
- **Access** detailed usage reports
- **Withdraw consent** for continued data collection

### Children's Rights:
- Children under 13 cannot create accounts without parental consent
- Children's data is stored separately and protected with additional security
- Parents have full administrative control over child accounts

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### We DO NOT:
- Sell or rent personal information to third parties
- Share children's personal information for marketing purposes
- Use children's data for targeted advertising

### We MAY share information:
- **With parental consent** - For educational or family purposes
- **Aggregated and anonymized data** - For research and app improvement
- **When required by law** - To comply with legal obligations
- **To protect safety** - In cases of emergency or abuse prevention
- **Service providers** - Encrypted and limited to necessary functions

### Third-Party Services:
- **SendGrid** - For email OTP verification (GDPR and CCPA compliant)
- **Expo/EAS** - For app updates and crash reporting
- **Analytics providers** - For app performance monitoring (no personal data)

## Data Security

### Security Measures:
- **End-to-end encryption** for data transmission
- **Secure password hashing** using industry standards
- **Regular security audits** and penetration testing
- **Access controls** limiting employee data access
- **Data minimization** - Only collect what's necessary

### Data Storage:
- **Encrypted databases** with regular backups
- **Geographic restrictions** - Data stored in secure, compliant regions
- **Regular deletion** of unnecessary data
- **Secure deletion** methods for permanent removal

## Data Retention

### Parent Accounts:
- Retained until account deletion request
- Inactive accounts deleted after 2 years of inactivity

### Child Accounts:
- Retained only as long as parent account is active
- Automatically deleted when parent account is deleted
- Parents can request immediate deletion at any time

### Educational Data:
- Learning progress retained for educational continuity
- Can be anonymized for research purposes (with consent)
- Deleted upon account termination

## International Data Transfers

If you are located outside of our primary service region, your data may be transferred to and processed in other countries. We ensure all transfers comply with applicable data protection laws and maintain adequate protection for your personal information.

## Your Rights

### You have the right to:
- **Access** your personal information and your children's information
- **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Delete** your account and associated data
- **Export** your data in a portable format
- **Withdraw consent** for data processing
- **Object** to certain data processing activities

### For Parents:
- Full access to all child account data
- Ability to modify or delete child information
- Control over data sharing preferences
- Detailed usage and activity reports
